# Fixed opening book for match.py.
#
# Every line is a short SAN line (2-4 ply) that a match starts from, and every line is
# played twice with the colours reversed. This exists because the engine is deterministic
# at move_temperature=0 and Stockfish is deterministic at a fixed node limit: without a
# book, a 100-game match is one game played 100 times.
#
# Lines are deliberately ordinary and roughly balanced - the point is to spread the games
# over different structures, not to test opening theory. Blank lines and #-comments are
# skipped; every line is validated as legal SAN when the book is loaded.
